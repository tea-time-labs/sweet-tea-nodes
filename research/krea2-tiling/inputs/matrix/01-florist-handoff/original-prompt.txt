Tall vertical editorial photograph for a neighborhood florist campaign. A florist in a dark green apron steps out of her shop and hands one wrapped bouquet of yellow ranunculus across the center to a cyclist in a cream jacket. Two adults only. Their hands meet around the brown paper wrapping, and the cyclist's other hand holds the blue bicycle's handlebar. Shop window with narrow wooden mullions, dappled rain on the pavement, soft cloudy daylight, candid medium-wide composition, clear layering from doorway to sidewalk.
