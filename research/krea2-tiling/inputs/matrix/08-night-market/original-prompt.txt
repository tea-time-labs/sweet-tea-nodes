Cinematic wide street campaign photograph of a lively Asian night market after rain. At center a market vendor in navy hands a single small kraft paper bag across the counter to one customer in a burnt-orange raincoat. A second customer in a cream coat waits beside her holding a folded umbrella at her side. Other shoppers remain tiny and out of focus down the lane. Rows of warm awning lights, slick paving reflecting magenta and amber, orderly market stalls receding diagonally, expressive poses and realistic hands.
